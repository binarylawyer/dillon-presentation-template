# 2. Tokenization

**What is being tokenized:** Each **LP membership interest** in **Troika Main Street QOF, LLC** is represented by a **Class A Security Token** issued on the **Metallicus XPR Network**, carrying all rights of a limited partnership interest (preferred return, profit share, exit proceeds). Separately, each of the 10 properties is represented by a **property NFT** on **Metal Blockchain** as an on-chain title/provenance/collateral registry, and quarterly distributions are denominated in **Metal Dollar (XMD)** stablecoin. *(Source: Business Plan IM, Blockchain Tokenization Strategy; QOF Op. Agr. §3.5, Art. 1, §4.3)*

**Platform/chain:** The **Metallicus Ecosystem** — **XPR Network** (token issuance) + **Metal Blockchain** (Layer-0 Avalanche fork; property NFTs) + **WebAuth Wallet** (investor interface, KYC/AML) + **XMD** (distributions). FedNow-certified infrastructure is cited. *(Source: Business Plan IM, Blockchain Tokenization Strategy)*

> **DECIDED — Metallicus is the platform.** The founders have confirmed **Metallicus (XPR Network / Metal Blockchain / WebAuth / XMD)** as the tokenization and distribution rail. The earlier alternative is retained here only as historical context, not an open question:
> - **Aegis Terra strategic memo (May 15, 2026)** had recommended ERC-3643 (T-REX) tokens on Polygon/Base/Arc with Metallicus only as a compliance subnet, and flagged that compliant US secondary trading must run through a **registered ATS** (tZERO, INX, Securitize Markets). *(Source: DillonSCStrategicAnalysis ["Aegis Terra"], pp. 1, 5–6)* — **superseded by the decision to use Metallicus.** The one carry-forward worth noting for counsel: confirm how compliant US secondary transfers are effected on the Metallicus stack (transfer-agent / ATS pathway), since that question is independent of which chain is chosen.

**Offering exemption & eligibility:** **Reg D Rule 506(c)** — **Accredited Investors only**, with KYC/AML and accreditation verification via the Metallicus WebAuth Wallet. *(Source: Business Plan IM Offering table & Investment Process; Project Team sheet)*

> **DECIDED — 506(c) (accredited investors only).** The offering is **Reg D Rule 506(c)**. Note that the QOF Operating Agreement §3.3 currently contains a leftover line authorizing the Manager to "accept investments below the minimum from **up to 35 non-accredited investors permitted under Rule 506(b)**." **This is a drafting error to delete** — non-accredited investors are not permitted in a 506(c) offering, and 506(b)/506(c) are mutually exclusive. Securities counsel should strike that clause and conform §3.3 to 506(c) before launch. *(Source: QOF Op. Agr. §3.3 vs. §8 & IM)*

**Lock-up / transfer restrictions:** No LP may transfer/assign/pledge interests or Class A Tokens without **prior written Manager consent** and compliance with: a **Regulation D 12-month lock-up from issuance (506(c))**; verification the transferee is accredited; OZ 2.0 holding requirements; all federal/state securities laws; and the Metallicus transfer-agent procedures. *(Source: QOF Op. Agr. Art. 8)*

**Secondary-liquidity plan:** IM describes "secondary market liquidity — token holders may transfer interests subject to Reg D lock-up periods and transfer restrictions" via the Metallicus platform, framed as a forward capability, **not a guarantee**; the Risk Factors state the secondary market is "nascent — no guarantee of liquidity prior to fund exit." *(Source: Business Plan IM, Token Structure & Liquidity Risks)* The Aegis Terra memo states a **registered ATS is mandatory** for compliant US secondary trading and Metal X "cannot serve that function." *(Source: DillonSCStrategicAnalysis p. 6)*
